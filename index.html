Five decades of trust, one family’s craft

In 1974 our founder opened a single workbench in Zaveri Bazaar with one rule: tell the customer the truth about the gold. We have never changed it.

Today three generations work side by side. The elders still judge colour and weight by eye and hand, while our designers use modern tools to draw, cast and finish pieces with a precision that was not possible when we began. Customers who bought their wedding jewellery from us now bring their own children.

  • Hallmarked gold. Every gold piece carries the BIS hallmark, and purity is tested in front of you.
  • Certified diamonds. Stones come with an independent grading report that you can verify.
  • Lifetime care. Cleaning, checks and honest exchange value for every piece you buy from us.
  1. 1974One workbench and a promise of honest weight and purity.
  2. 1991The first showroom opens, and the family begins offering custom design.
  3. 2008The second generation adds a diamond studio and a bridal design team.
  4. TodayThree generations, one standard, and a showroom that feels like visiting family.
